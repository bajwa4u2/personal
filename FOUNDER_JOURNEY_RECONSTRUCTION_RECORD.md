# Founder Journey Reconstruction Record

STATUS = REVIEW-WORTHY IMPLEMENTATION

## Scope

This record covers only the canonical founder Journey experience at `/journey`.
The legacy `personal/bajwa` estate remains read-only reference material and is
not a runtime dependency.

## Experience architecture

Journey uses one continuous passage across changing ground rather than a
stacked biography, resume, or seven-part section template. The visitor moves
through documented conditions: Pakistan Army Signals (1991–2000), an honest
2000–2003 record gap, Oman operating work (2003–2022), independent platform
formation (2022–2025), and the open Aura Platform present (2026–now).

Home remains responsible for portrait-led presence, founder video, current-work
introduction and initial relationship. Journey deliberately does not repeat
the portrait or video. Writing remains a parallel authored dimension and is
linked only where it naturally meets the present.

## Material used

- verified founder chronology and role/place material;
- approved Pakistan Army Signals and Oman operating context;
- approved current Aura Platform and Bajwa Writes relationship;
- factual period labels and the explicit 2000–2003 record boundary.

## Material rejected

- legacy Journey/Story/Work page shells and visual grammar;
- founder video and portrait duplication;
- CV/resume records, unsupported achievements and invented historical imagery;
- decorative military theatre, retrospective Aura mythology and internal
  development language.

## Interaction and motion

The page is naturally scrollable. Lightweight reveal hooks enhance movement but
all meaningful content exists in the initial document and remains complete in
reduced-motion mode. The founder shell menu is keyboard and touch operable.

## Responsive QA

Sequential visual traversal was completed at 1440, 1024, 390, 360 and 320
widths, including full-page and reduced-motion captures. The corrected layout
uses single-column mobile composition, preserves Oman depth, and avoids
horizontal overflow.

## Routing and discovery

`/journey` is the only canonical Journey document. `/story`, `/work`, and
`/contact` are compatibility redirects handled by the Worker; their clean
visitor shells are not published. Legacy `.html` routes map one-hop to the
approved canonical Journey, Writing or Start a conversation destinations.

Journey has its own canonical metadata and ProfilePage/Person relationship,
with no founder video duplication. Build verification checks canonical route
markers, retired route status, legacy isolation and the no-video invariant.
