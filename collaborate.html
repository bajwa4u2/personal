Different people contribute in different ways.

Aura Platform LLC is an operating company with three live products. There are a small number of ways serious people advance it.

Some advance it by building and operating.

Some by investing.

Some by advising.

Some by engaging the company directly, or by joining the long-horizon thesis.

This page maps those opportunities.
