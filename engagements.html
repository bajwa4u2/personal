Engagement

A narrow door for founder-led work.

Select paid engagements are accepted each year — strategic advisory, architecture review, and specialized work. Scoped, deliberate, and chosen for fit.

Posture

The work of Aura Platform LLC comes first. A small number of outside engagements are taken each year, where the problem is institutional, the scope is defined, and the fit is clear. This is a door, not a storefront — the founder remains the constant; the engagement is the exception.

Engagements

Three forms of engagement.

Each is bounded, fee-based, and led by the founder. None is open-ended. Scope and terms are set per engagement, in correspondence — there is no rate card and no booking widget.

Strategic advisory

Institutional · operational · governance

Advisory on institutional strategy, operations, governance models, platform direction, and execution — drawn from three decades of operating and building. Engagements answer a defined question, not an open relationship.

Not coaching. Not mentoring. Not fractional employment.

Architecture review

Systems · platforms · continuity

A structured examination of software systems, platforms, governance models, execution architectures, continuity systems, and operational designs. The outcome is a written record of findings and direction.

Deliverable-oriented. Not hourly consulting.

Custom engagement

Specialized · scoped · institutional

Specialized engagements that do not fit a predefined category. Where the problem is institutional and the scope can be made clear, the form is defined together before any engagement begins.

Defined per problem. Accepted by fit.
