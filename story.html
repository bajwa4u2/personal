In brief

Muhammad Sakhawat Bajwa is the founder and CEO of Aura Platform LLC — an operator who built the company he now runs. A start in military communications, then nearly two decades in Oman across infrastructure, operations, and executive leadership, turned into software. The company rests on one belief: communication, execution, and continuity should not be treated as separate problems. It expresses that belief through one ecosystem on a shared substrate: Institution → Aura, Operation → Orchestrate, Continuity → Bajwa Writes.

Transcript

Hello, my name is Muhammad Sakhawat Bajwa, and I am the founder of Aura Platform LLC.

I began my career in military communications and later spent nearly two decades in Oman working in infrastructure, operations, and executive leadership roles. Over time, I became increasingly interested in the communication, coordination, and execution challenges that organizations face every day.

That experience led me to build Aura Platform LLC.

Today, the company consists of three connected products: Aura, Orchestrate, and Bajwa Writes.

Aura focuses on accountable communication for individuals, organizations, and institutions.

Orchestrate focuses on operational execution, helping organizations move from communication and planning to measurable outcomes.

Bajwa Writes focuses on continuity and preservation, helping authors and institutions preserve knowledge and written works over time.

All three products are built around the same belief: communication, execution, and continuity should not be treated as separate problems.

Thank you.

M S Bajwa

Founder & CEO, Aura Platform LLC.
