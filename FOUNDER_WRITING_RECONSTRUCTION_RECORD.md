# Founder Writing Reconstruction Record

STATUS = REVIEW-WORTHY IMPLEMENTATION

## Governing experience

Writing is composed as a movement from question to tension to thought to
written form and sustained inquiry. These are transitions in reading posture,
not five named page sections. The opening enters a question directly; later
thought acquires measure and margin; the books appear as evidence that an
inquiry continued into durable form.

## Authority

The three book objects and summaries come from the approved static
`personal/bajwa/data/books.json` manifest and were migrated previously into the
clean estate. No essay was inferred from the empty essay manifest. Vision 2050
is represented cautiously as an authored long-horizon question, not as company
strategy or a factual forecast. No text is presented as a quotation.

## Boundaries

Home presence, Journey life/work movement and Bajwa Writes publication remain
separate. Writing does not repeat the founder portrait, video, chronology or
Home book stack. Bajwa Writes is reached as the complete publication world and
is not cloned here.

## Visual and interaction model

The page uses changing reading measure, restrained editorial fields, marginal
relationships and controlled book-cover presence. The three books are
deliberately offset rather than presented as equal catalogue cards. The mobile
layout is independently composed into a readable single-column sequence. The
founder menu remains keyboard/touch operable and reduced motion removes only
nonessential transitions.

## QA and deployment

Local and live full-page renders were inspected at 1440, 1024, 390, 360 and
320 widths, plus reduced-motion 390. Opening, thought progression, works,
horizon, closing, footer and mobile reflow were reviewed. Build and verify
passed; the route is deployed at `https://bajwa.auraplatform.org/writing`.

The first visual pass was rejected for excessive scene height and oversized
display type. A density correction reduced viewport ownership and display scale
while retaining readable line-height and mobile breathing. The corrected live
renders were inspected again before handoff.
